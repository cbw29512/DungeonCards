## Current public-content baseline

The public `coc-7e` Card Catalog contains **93 definitions**:

- 9 verified procedure cards;
- 24 original public-safe weapons;
- 24 original public-safe rituals and unnatural effects;
- 24 original public-safe creatures and NPCs;
- 12 original public-safe premade Investigators.

The Investigator workspace also contains **24 original occupation packages** used by the builder. Occupations are currently directory/builder records rather than standalone Card Platform definitions.

These are original DM Forge records. They are not copied official Chaosium catalogs and are not evidence that official paid-book creatures, spells, occupations, or scenarios have been implemented.

## Accuracy boundary

Rules accuracy remains the primary acceptance criterion. Original content volume does not certify a rule implementation.

Current rules and calculators remain governed by [`docs/COC_RULES_ACCURACY.md`](./COC_RULES_ACCURACY.md). Official paid-book catalogs, scenarios, artwork, logos, protected statistics, and substantial rulebook prose remain excluded without permission.

## Licensing boundary

Public development may continue with original catalog content, public-domain inspiration expressed through original statistics and prose, concise source-audited procedures, and private user-owned imports.

The repository does not publish official Chaosium scenarios, protected catalog records, artwork, logos, copied tables, or substantial rulebook text.
